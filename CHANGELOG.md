# Changelog: Dogany LifeKit

## 2.47.2

Meal cards, ledger and cooking screens change, plus bug fixes.

- Meal cards show a compact nutrition summary and food log.
- The ledger accounts for this month's spending and separates fact questions.
- Cooking steps appear in execution order with markers for each dish.
- Bugs in goal consultations, cooking, deposit classification, appointment
  times and annual subscription reminders are fixed.
- Requires Dogany 2.7.0 or later.

## 2.46.2

The weekly retro and the health reset change, plus bug fixes.

- The weekly retro opens with the week's big events and asks about missed
  daily retros one at a time.
- Snoozing a task never triggers an extra question.
- The health reset also clears workout gear answers.
- Bugs in snoozed task alerts and ledger deposit questions are fixed.
- Requires Dogany 2.7.0 or later.

## 2.46.1

Starting the ledger asks things in a clearer order with clearer screens, and
updates get lighter.

- Ledger: fact questions come together up front, and the big-shopping limit
  shows where its number comes from.
- Ledger: incoming-money questions can be answered with a button or by typing.
- Ledger messages use the form of address you set.
- Updates download fewer files.
- Requires Dogany 2.7.0 or later.

## 2.46.0

The ledger now starts inside Lifekit, and schedules, meals and health records
get closer care.

- Ledger: start from a transaction file or screenshot; three months of cash
  flow lead to an amount to save or cut.
- Schedules: appointment reminders and reviews show travel, people and goals;
  a missed weekly review continues on Monday.
- Outings and cooking: restaurant and outing plans, and several dishes cooked
  together with steps and timers.
- Records: incorrect records can be edited and undone. Fixed errors when adding
  food to a meal and appointment reminders splitting apart.
- Requires Dogany 2.7.0 or later.

## 2.40.1

Public-release hygiene, no behavior change.

## 2.37.1

First public release.

- Calendar: tasks, appointments, stays and recurring routines in plain
  language, with optional Google Calendar sync.
- Travel: travel time between appointments. The agent asks about missing
  places, routes and starting points, and looks up routes between places.
- Places and people: a place book that learns from names, addresses and map
  links. A relationship book with nickname lookup, keep-in-touch reminders and
  pre-meeting briefings.
- Health: meal logging with a daily nutrition card and optional targets,
  workout logging, InBody body-composition capture, and pain notes linked to
  workouts.
- Goals: a guided goal conversation that turns a goal into a weekly plan,
  routines and calendar blocks.
- Reflection: a morning brief, a nightly daily retro, a weekly review on
  request, and episodes that group related records into one experience.
- Money: spending entries with per-category and per-payment-method views.
- First-use setup: on install, choose to start with calendar management or
  with meals, workouts and body measurements.
- User-facing text in Korean and English.
